AeroPress

Fast, forgiving, and travel-friendly. The AeroPress works with a range of recipes from inverted to upright. Here are champion and classic approaches.

James Hoffmann's Ultimate AeroPress

James Hoffmann – 2019 Championship Intermediate
Ratio 11g : 200g
Water temp 100°C (boiling)
Grind Fine
Method Inverted
  1. Set up AeroPress inverted. Add 11g fine coffee.
  2. Add 200g boiling water, stir 2–3 times. Start timer.
  3. At 2:00, attach cap with rinsed filter. Flip onto mug at 2:10.
  4. Press gently over 30–45 seconds. Total brew ~2:45.

Standard AeroPress Method

Classic approach Beginner
Ratio 14g : 200g
Water temp 85–90°C
Grind Medium-fine
Time 1:30 steep + 30s press
  1. Add coffee, then water. Stir 10 seconds.
  2. Steep 1 minute. Attach cap, press slowly over 30 seconds.

Inverted Method (Alan Adler Style)

Alan Adler – Creator of AeroPress Beginner
Ratio 17g : 220g
Water temp 80–85°C
Grind Fine
  1. Inverted: plunger at 4. Add coffee, water, stir 10s.
  2. Steep 1 min. Cap and flip, press slowly.

Cold Brew AeroPress

Beginner
Ratio 20g : 200g cold water
Grind Coarse
Time 12–24 hr in fridge
  1. Inverted. Coarse grind, room-temp or cold water. Steep 12–24 hours in fridge.
  2. Press and dilute to taste with water or ice.
