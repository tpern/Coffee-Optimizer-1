Espresso

Pressure, grind, and time in balance. These guides cover dialing in, championship-style parameters, and professional workflows.

Dialing In Basics

Beginner
Dose 18–20g
Yield 36–40g (1:2)
Time 25–30s
Temp 93–96°C
  1. Start with 18g dose, 36g yield, 25–30s. Grind to hit time first.
  2. Too fast → grind finer. Too slow → grind coarser.
  3. Then adjust ratio: more yield for lighter body, less for intensity.

Championship Espresso Profile

World Barista Championship standards Advanced
Dose 18–22g
Yield 1:2–1:2.5
Time 28–35s
Pre-infusion Optional 5–8s
  1. Pre-infusion at low pressure, then ramp to 9 bar.
  2. Consistent tamp, WDT for even extraction. Track extraction % if possible.
