Kalita Wave

Flat-bottom design promotes even extraction and a consistent bed. These recipes cover standard pour technique, championship-style, and a simple beginner method.

Flat-Bottom Pour Technique

Intermediate
Ratio 24g : 400g
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium
Time 3:30–4:00
  1. Rinse filter. Bloom 50g, 45s. Pour in concentric circles to 200g, then 400g.
  2. Flat bed helps even extraction. Gentle swirl if needed.

Championship Kalita Recipe

Advanced
Ratio 20g : 300g
Water temp 92–94°C
Grind Medium-fine
Pours 4–5 equal pours
  1. Structured pours with 30–45s between each. Maintain flat bed.
  2. Total time 3:30–4:00. Adjust grind for flow rate.

Beginner Kalita Method

Beginner
Ratio 15g : 250g
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium
  1. Bloom 30g, 30s. Two pours: to 125g, then 250g. Simple and repeatable.
