V60 / Pour Over

The Hario V60’s spiral ribs and large hole allow controlled flow and clarity. These recipes from world champions and educators will help you get the most from the V60.

James Hoffmann's V60 Technique

James Hoffmann Intermediate
Ratio 30g : 500g (1:16.6)
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium-fine
Time 3:30–4:00
  1. Bloom with 60g water for 45 seconds.
  2. Pour in circles to 300g, then to 500g. Gentle swirl after final pour.
  3. Let draw down. Total time 3:30–4:00.
Use water off the boil; grind medium-fine. Avoid stirring the bloom too aggressively.

Tetsu Kasuya's 4:6 Method

Tetsu Kasuya – 2016 World Brewers Cup Winner Advanced
Ratio 20g : 300g
Water temp 92–93°C
Grind Medium-coarse
Pours 5 pours (40% sweetness, 60% strength)
  1. First pour: 40% of water (120g) in 2 equal parts to control sweetness.
  2. Wait 45 seconds between pours.
  3. Remaining 60% (180g) in 3 equal parts to control strength. 45s between each.
More pours in the first 40% = sweeter. More pours in the 60% = stronger.

Scott Rao's V60 Method

Scott Rao Advanced
Ratio 1:16–1:17
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium-fine
  1. Focus on even extraction with controlled agitation.
  2. Single continuous pour or few pours with gentle swirl to level bed.
  3. Minimize channeling; flat bed at end of brew.
Scott Rao – The Coffee Roaster’s Companion

One-Cup V60 (Beginner Friendly)

Simple daily method Beginner
Ratio 15g : 250g
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium
Time ~2:30–3:00
  1. Rinse filter, add 15g coffee. Bloom with 30g water, 30 seconds.
  2. Pour to 150g in circles, then to 250g.
  3. Light swirl, let draw down.
Easy to remember: one tablespoon per 100ml. Great for a single cup.
