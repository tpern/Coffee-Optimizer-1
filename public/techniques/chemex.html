Chemex

Thick filters and a classic design give a clean, tea-like cup. These recipes cover standard ratios, advanced techniques, and large batches.

Classic Chemex Ratio

Beginner
Ratio 30g : 500g
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium-coarse
Time 4–5 min
  1. Rinse thick Chemex filter. Add 30g coffee. Bloom 45s with ~60g water.
  2. Pour in circles to 300g, then to 500g. Gentle swirl. Let draw down.
Chemex filters absorb more oils—use a slightly coarser grind than V60.

Large Batch Chemex

Intermediate
Ratio 60g : 1000g
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium-coarse
Time 6–8 min
  1. Scale up ratio. Bloom 90g, 45s. Multiple pours to 600g, then 1000g.
  2. Expect longer draw-down; keep bed flat and even.
