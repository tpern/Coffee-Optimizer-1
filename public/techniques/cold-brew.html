Cold Brew

Low acidity, smooth body, and easy to batch. Choose long immersion, Japanese iced (hot bloom), or a shorter quick cold brew.

Immersion Cold Brew (12–24 hours)

Beginner
Ratio 1:8 (e.g. 100g : 800g)
Grind Coarse
Time 12–24 hr (fridge)
Water Cold or room temp
  1. Coarse grind, add water. Stir to saturate. Cover and refrigerate 12–24 hours.
  2. Strain through filter or cheesecloth. Dilute 1:1 with water or serve over ice.
Concentrate keeps 1–2 weeks in fridge. Dilute to taste.

Japanese Iced Coffee (Hot Bloom)

Hot brew onto ice Intermediate
Ratio 30g : 200g hot + 200g ice
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium-fine
Method V60 or similar onto ice
  1. Put 200g ice in server. Brew 30g coffee with 200g hot water (1:6.6) directly onto ice.
  2. Hot extraction locks in brightness; ice dilutes and chills. Serve immediately.

Quick Cold Brew (4–6 hours)

Beginner
Ratio 1:8
Grind Medium-coarse (finer than 24hr)
Time 4–6 hr room temp
  1. Finer grind speeds extraction. Room temp 4–6 hours, then strain and chill.
  2. Good for same-day cold coffee without overnight wait.
