French Press

Full immersion, rich body, and minimal gear. These recipes range from James Hoffmann’s no-plunge technique to classic and strong styles.

James Hoffmann's French Press

James Hoffmann Beginner
Ratio 30g : 500g
Water temp 100°C (boiling)
Grind Coarse
Time 4 min steep, no plunge
  1. Add coarse coffee. Pour all water, ensure all grounds are saturated.
  2. Wait 4 minutes. Skim crust and stir. Let grounds settle 5–8 minutes.
  3. Place lid so plunger sits on top of liquid (do not plunge). Pour gently.
No plunge = less silt and cleaner cup. Coarse grind is essential.

Classic French Press

Beginner
Ratio 1:15 (e.g. 34g : 500g)
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Coarse
Time 4 min, then plunge
  1. Add coffee, pour water, stir. Steep 4 minutes.
  2. Plunge slowly. Pour and serve.

Strong French Press

Beginner
Ratio 1:12 (e.g. 42g : 500g)
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Coarse
Time 4–5 min
  1. Higher dose, same volume for a bolder cup. Steep 4–5 min, plunge and pour.
