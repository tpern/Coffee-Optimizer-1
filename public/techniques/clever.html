Clever Dripper

Immersion plus filtered draw-down. Steep, then place on a mug to drain. These recipes cover standard, extended steep, and a hybrid pour-over style.

Standard Clever Recipe

Beginner
Ratio 25g : 400g
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium
Steep 3–4 min, then drain
  1. Add coffee, pour water, stir. Steep 3–4 minutes with valve closed.
  2. Place on mug to open valve. Draw-down ~1 min. Remove dripper.

Extended Steep Clever

Beginner
Ratio 25g : 400g
Water temp 90–93°C
Grind Medium-coarse
Steep 5–6 min
  1. Longer steep for more body. Coarser grind to avoid over-extraction.
  2. Drain when ready. Slower draw-down is normal.

Hybrid Pour-Over Style

Intermediate
Ratio 20g : 320g
Water temp 93–96°C
Grind Medium-fine
Method Bloom + 2 pours, then drain
  1. Bloom 40g, 30s. Pour to 160g, wait 30s. Pour to 320g. Steep 1 min, then drain.
  2. Combines immersion body with more clarity from multiple pours.
